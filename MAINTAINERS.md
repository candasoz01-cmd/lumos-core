For ordinary project questions, contact the maintainer through a GitHub issue.
Security reports must not be posted publicly; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What "maintainer" means here

A maintainer:

- lands changes on `main` under the three-gate regime in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#merge-gate)
- is the human authority for gate 3 (explicit human approval)
- decides scope: what belongs in the public OSS foundation and what stays in the
  private Lumos layers (see CONTRIBUTING.md, "Public repository boundary")
- owns triage of incoming issues and pull requests

Automated contributors (Cursor agents, `github-actions[bot]`, and similar) appear
in the commit history but are **not** maintainers and cannot satisfy the human
approval gate.
